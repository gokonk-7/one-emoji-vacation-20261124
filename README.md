A single emoji standing in for the trip of their dreams — entered in make.fun's "One Emoji, One Dream Vacation" bounty, where a judge model compares entries two at a time and picks the one that most makes a stranger want to book the flight.

**The entry: 🏄** — a surfer riding a wave. Not the obvious beach umbrella or island the whole field submits, but one specific dream: chasing waves somewhere warm. Sun, sea, motion — a postcard at a glance, personal instead of generic, and a fresh pick among beaches, islands, and planes.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```

`main.ts` (Node 22+) validates the emoji against the gate — 1 to 8 UTF-8 characters, not only whitespace — and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
